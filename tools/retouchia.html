About Retouchia

Retouchia harnesses deep learning algorithms to restore old, blurred, or damaged vintage photographs. It automatically removes scratches, enhances blurry facial features, and upscales resolution up to crisp 4K.

Key Features

  • Automated vintage photo restoration
  • 4K resolution upscaler & sharpener
  • Deep learning facial feature recovery
  • Scratch, dust, and digital noise removal
  • AI historic photo colorization
