About us

Built to help you find the right tool, not just another list.

Flexora.Ai is a young project — this site is still an early build, not a finished product. Here's what we're trying to do and how we're doing it.

What we're building

An AI tool discovery platform that goes beyond a directory: search, comparisons, a Tool Finder quiz, free browser utilities, and ready-made workflows — all built around one promise, helping people find the right AI tool for the job.

How we're different

We don't charge small AI companies just to get listed. We'd rather send real users first and build a relationship from there. Every submitted tool is reviewed by a human before it goes live.

Who's building it

One founder, working with AI assistants for research, planning, coding, and review — starting small on a $0 budget with GitHub Pages, and expanding step by step.

Where things stand

Right now the site is a working prototype: the layout, Tool Finder, comparisons, and Word Counter are functional, but the tool listings are demo data and there's no backend yet. Real tools, accounts, and automation come in later phases.
