THE STORY

Why this exists

Most "AI tool directories" are the same thing wearing different colors — a wall of logos, sorted by who paid the most to be at the top. Flexora.Ai started from a simpler question: if you actually need a tool for a specific task today, which of these hundreds of options is worth your time?

So instead of just a list, this became a small set of tools for finding tools — search and comparison, a quiz that matches by task and budget, ready-made workflows for common jobs, and a couple of free utilities that need no account at all.

It's still early. There's no backend, no team, and no outside funding — just one founder, working with AI assistants for research, coding, and review, building this in public on GitHub Pages.

HOW WE'RE DIFFERENT

What makes this not another directory

01

Human-reviewed, not scraped

Every submitted tool is checked by hand before it goes live. No bulk-imported, unverified listings padding out the numbers.

02

Built to match, not just list

The Tool Finder scores tools against your actual task and budget — the goal is the right tool for you, not just a big logo wall.

03

Free utilities included

Not every useful thing needs to be a "tool submission" — some things, like the Word Counter, are just built in and free.

The directory, Tool Finder, comparisons, and Word Counter are functional. Real tools are being added; there's no backend yet — everything runs on GitHub Pages.

PHASE 2 — NEXT
