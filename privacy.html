Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Flexora.Ai is currently an early-stage prototype. This page describes our intended approach to privacy as we build the real site — treat it as a draft, not a finalized legal policy, until we launch fully.

What we plan to collect

Basic usage data (pages visited, searches run) to understand what's useful, and any information you choose to submit through forms (like the tool submission or contact forms), once those are connected to a real backend.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data to third parties. We won't share your contact details with tool companies without your consent.

Cookies

The current prototype does not set tracking cookies. If we add analytics later, we'll update this page and disclose what's used.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@flexora.ai.
