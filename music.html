How to Add Songs & Custom Thumbnails

Add files directly via GitHub. FlexoraAi auto-detects, creates waveforms, and publishes them in minutes.

1
Open GitHub Repo

Visit flexora-ai/flexora-ai.github.io and open the assets/audio/ folder.

2
Upload Audio Files

Upload your .mp3, .wav, .ogg, or .flac files.

3
Add Custom Thumbnail

Upload a matching image with the same name, e.g. my-song.png or my-song.jpg. (If omitted, we generate neon neon album art automatically!)

4
Instant Streaming

Commit your upload. FlexoraAi immediately indexes the file, formats titles, and streams it live.
